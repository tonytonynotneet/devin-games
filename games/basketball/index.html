📖 Rules

  • First to 11 points, or lead when the 90-second clock runs out
  • Tied? Sudden-death overtime — first basket wins
  • Hold Action to charge; release in the green zone for a swish!
  • Shots from beyond the 3pt line are worth 3 points
  • Without the ball, Action is a steal
  • Jump into a shot to block it
  • A quick tap is a fake. P pauses
  • 📱 On touch devices, use the on-screen buttons
