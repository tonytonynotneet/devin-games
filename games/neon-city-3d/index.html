HOW TO PLAY

Left half — virtual stick to move / drive (push up = forward)

Right half — drag to rotate the camera & aim

A button — enter/exit car · talk to the fixer (mission start)

B button — attack / shoot (hold for autofire, aim assist on)

C button — cycle weapons

Rules — crimes raise your ☆ wanted level; cops hunt you down. Get caught = BUSTED (station), get killed = WASTED (hospital). Do fixer missions for cash. The game saves automatically.

2 players — open the koto link on one phone and the zuza link on the other; you spawn together in the same city.
